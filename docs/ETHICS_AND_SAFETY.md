# AMADEUS Safety, Ethics & Compliance Policy

## Safeguards
- Living Artist Restrictions: Prompts targeting specific living artists by name are intercepted and redirected to stylistic descriptors.
- Melody Plagiarism Filtering: Realtime n-gram pitch detector blocks any generation exhibiting greater than 8 contiguous notes matching licensed source references.
- Watermarking & Attribution: Output audio embeds AudioSeal watermarks and generates a cryptographic provenance manifest conforming to EU AI Act Article 52 disclosure requirements.
- Rights Protection: Complete separation between user training data and licensed proprietary assets.
